Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026 (updated from November 18, 2026) · Wimzi Games LLC

This policy explains what information Wimzi Games LLC ("Wimzi Games", "we", "us") collects when you play our mobile games, including Wimzi Solitaire, and use our related websites and support (together, the "Games"), how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. Wimzi Games LLC is the company responsible for your information (the "controller" under EU and UK law). If you have questions, email support@wimzigames.com.

The short version

  • You can play without giving us your name, email address or phone number. Your account is anonymous unless you choose to link it.
  • We use your information to run the Games, save your progress, provide leaderboards and friends features, answer support, keep play fair, and show ads that keep the Games free.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money. Ad partners may use your advertising identifier for personalized ads only where you allow it, and you can turn this off at any time.
  • You can delete your account in the Game at any time (Profile → Delete account).

1. Information we collect

Information created when you play

  • Game account identifiers. When you first open a Game, an anonymous player account is created for you automatically. It has a random player ID and a short player code (for example, K7MP2Q) that you can share with support or friends.
  • Game progress and activity. Your coins, wins, scores, best times, streaks, levels, settings, unlocked items, sticker album, missions, races, duels, gifts, event progress and, if offered, rewards points, so your progress is saved, can be restored, and is calculated fairly. For some modes (for example, the Daily Deal) we receive the moves you made so our servers can check that the score is genuine.
  • Profile and social features. The display name and avatar you choose, your friends list, friend requests, the pre-written messages, gifts and challenges you send, and your leaderboard and race results.
  • Country or region. The country set in your device's region setting (not your GPS location). We use it to show events and offers available in your country and, if you leave the setting on, to show your country's flag on the World leaderboard. You can turn the flag off in Settings.
  • Device and app information. Device model, operating system and version, platform (iOS or Android), app version, language setting, time zone, and the dates you first and last played.
  • Device integrity (Android). On Android, Google Play tells us whether the Game appears to be a genuine copy running on a genuine device. We use this result only to detect cheating and fraud.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, a push token for your device and your notification choices (for example, which alerts you want and quiet hours) so we can send the alerts you asked for.
  • Purchases. If you buy something, Apple or Google processes the payment. We receive the product, a transaction or order ID, the store, the date and whether it was refunded, so we can deliver your purchase, restore it and handle refunds. We never receive your card or bank details.

Information from account linking (optional)

  • If you link your account with Sign in with Apple or Google Play Games, we receive an account identifier from Apple or Google so you can restore your progress on another device. We do not ask Apple for your name or email address. Google Play Games may share your Play Games player ID and gamer name according to your Google settings.

Information you give us

  • Support requests. If you contact us from the in-game Help screen, we receive the topic and message you write, your player code, and your device model, operating system, app version and language, and we store them with your account so we can reply in the Game. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. To help us answer quickly and in your language, our staff may use an AI service provider to translate your message and draft a reply; a member of our team reviews every reply before it is sent.
  • Reports. If you report another player (for example, for an offensive name or cheating), we receive the reason, where you reported it, and the player codes involved.
  • Birthday (optional). If you add your birthday in Profile, we store the month and day only (never the year) to send you a birthday gift each year.
  • Friend invites. When you share an invite, the link contains your friend code. If someone installs a Game from your invite, the app store may pass that code to the Game so the two of you are connected automatically, and we record that the player joined through a friend invite.
  • Shared pictures. If you share a result picture, it is created on your device and shared through your device's share menu. We do not receive the picture or who you share it with.

Players can send each other only pre-written messages, gifts and challenges. There is no free-text chat.

Information collected by our service providers

  • Analytics. Google Analytics for Firebase collects information about how the Games are used (for example, games started and won, screens opened, ads watched), an app instance identifier, device information and approximate location (country or region, derived from IP address).
  • Crash reports. Firebase Crashlytics (Google) collects information when a Game crashes or has a serious error: the device model and operating system, app version, the state of the app at the time and your random player ID, so we can find and fix the problem.
  • Advertising. Our Games show ads through Google AdMob. Other ad networks can compete through AdMob to show an ad (currently Unity Ads). Google and these networks may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device information and ad interactions, to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. Where required, we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.
  • Downloads. When the Game downloads new pictures or content, the content delivery service receives your IP address and basic request information, as any website does.

2. How we use information

  • To run the Games: create your account, save and sync your progress, restore it on a new device, deliver purchases and rewards. (EU/UK legal basis: performance of our contract with you.)
  • To provide social and competitive features: leaderboards, friends, gifts, duels, races, events and the World leaderboard. (Contract.)
  • To answer support requests and reports. Some players may be assigned a personal support host; that host can see your player code and the support messages you send from the Game. (Contract; legitimate interests.)
  • To keep play fair and safe: check scores, detect cheating, fraud and abuse, review reports and enforce our Terms. (Legitimate interests; legal obligations.)
  • To understand and improve the Games and fix problems. (Legitimate interests.)
  • To send notifications you have allowed. (Consent, which you can withdraw in your device settings or in the Game.)
  • To show ads that keep the Games free: contextual ads (legitimate interests) and personalized ads only where you allow them (consent).
  • To comply with law and respond to lawful requests. (Legal obligations.)

Automated checks. Our servers automatically check scores and game results (for example, by replaying the moves of a Daily Deal). A result that fails the check is not added to the leaderboard. If you think this happened by mistake, contact support and a person will review it. We do not make other decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing.

3. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only:

  • With service providers that run parts of the Games for us under contracts that limit how they use it:
    • Unity Technologies: player accounts, cloud saves, leaderboards, friends, server scripts and content downloads, and Unity Ads (privacy policy)
    • Google: Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging, AdMob, Google Play and Play Games (privacy policy; how Google uses information from apps that use its services)
    • Apple: App Store, payments, Sign in with Apple and push notifications (privacy policy)
    • Cloudflare: hosting for our website, staff tools and store purchase checks (privacy policy)
    • Anthropic: an AI service that helps our support team translate messages and draft replies, and translates in-game text; it receives the content of support messages and the related game details, not your payment information (privacy policy)
    • Our email provider, to send and receive support email.
  • With other players, limited to your display name, avatar, player code (if you share it), country flag (if turned on), scores, race and duel results, and friends activity.
  • For legal reasons, when required by law or to protect the rights, property and safety of our players, Wimzi Games or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger, financing or sale of the company or a Game, subject to this policy.
  • With your direction or consent, for example when you link your account or share an invite.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Ads and tracking: where your location requires it (for example, the EEA, UK, Switzerland and some U.S. states), review or change your choices at any time in the Game under Profile → Privacy Choices, including opting out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your information for targeted advertising. You can also use your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID).
  • Notifications: turn them off in the Game's Settings or your device settings.
  • Country flag: turn it off in Settings.
  • Display name: change it any time on the Profile screen. Birthday: change it once a year in Profile, or ask support to correct it.
  • Delete your account: in the Game, go to Profile → Delete account; it takes effect immediately. Or follow the steps on our account deletion page. Deleting the app alone does not delete server data, and deleting your account does not cancel a subscription (cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings).
  • Access, correction, portability and other requests: email support@wimzigames.com with your player code (Profile → Player Code).

Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA, UK, Switzerland, Brazil, California and other U.S. states), you may have the right to know what information we hold about you and receive a copy, to correct it, to delete it, to receive it in a portable format, to object to or restrict certain uses, to withdraw consent, to opt out of targeted advertising and the "sale" or "sharing" of your information, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

How we handle requests. Because accounts are anonymous, we verify requests using your player code and, where needed, details only the account holder would know. You can use an authorized agent where the law allows; we may ask the agent for proof that you authorized them. We respond within the time the law requires (usually within one month in the EEA and UK, and within 45 days in U.S. states). If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with the subject "Appeal"; we will answer your appeal within the time the law requires and, if we still decline, tell you how to contact your state attorney general or data protection authority. We will not discriminate against you for using your rights.

In the last 12 months we collected the following categories of personal information, from you, your device and our service providers, for the purposes in Section 2:

  • Identifiers: player ID and code, display name, device and advertising identifiers, push token, account linking identifiers, IP address (through our service providers), and your email address if you email us.
  • Commercial information: purchase and subscription records.
  • Internet or other electronic network activity: gameplay, app usage, ad interactions and crash information.
  • Approximate geolocation: country or region (from your device setting or IP address). We do not collect precise location.
  • Other information you choose to give us: birthday month and day, support messages and reports.

We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by U.S. state laws, and we do not use or disclose any information to infer characteristics about you. Identifiers and network activity may be shared with advertising partners for targeted advertising, which some state laws call a "sale" or "sharing"; you can opt out as described in Section 4. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16. Our Games are mobile apps and do not receive browser Global Privacy Control signals; please use Privacy Choices in the Game or your device's ad settings, which we treat as your opt-out.

Nevada residents: we do not sell covered information as defined by Nevada law. You may still send a request to support@wimzigames.com with the subject "Nevada opt-out".

6. Retention

We keep game account data while your account is active and for up to 24 months after you last play, then delete or anonymize it. Purchase records are kept for as long as we need them for tax, accounting and refund purposes (usually up to 7 years). Support messages, emails and reports are kept for up to 24 months. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. Analytics data is kept according to our Firebase settings (up to 14 months). When you delete your account, your game account is deleted immediately and the analytics records linked to it in our own copies are anonymized within 30 days: the activity stays only as anonymous statistics that can no longer be linked to you. We may keep limited information longer where the law requires it or to resolve disputes and prevent fraud.

7. Children

Our Games are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, such as encrypted connections, access controls and limiting which staff can see player information. No system is perfectly secure. If a security incident affects your information, we will notify you and the authorities where the law requires.

9. International transfers

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries whose laws may differ from those where you live. Where required, transfers from the EEA, UK and Switzerland are protected by measures such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the provider's certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will update the date above and, if the changes are significant, tell you in the Game before they take effect.
